Safety 1 mg/mL is the IM strength, never an IV push with a pulse. AHA 2025 warns of push-dose errors: label syringes, read back doses.

Exam tip AHA 2025: asystole/PEA, epinephrine as soon as feasible; VF/pVT, epinephrine after the 2nd shock, amiodarone or lidocaine after the 3rd. No routine magnesium, calcium or bicarbonate in arrest.

Drip calculations

Every drip problem uses the same three steps: find the concentration in the bag, find the hourly pump rate, then convert to the unit the order uses. Write the units at each step; most errors are a missed conversion (mg to mcg, or hours to minutes).

StepFormula
ConcentrationDrug in the bag ÷ bag volume (1 mg = 1,000 mcg)
Weight-based ratemL/kg/h × weight in kg = mL/h
Dose per hourmL/h × concentration (mcg/mL) = mcg/h
Dose per minutemcg/h ÷ 60 = mcg/min
Per kilogrammcg/min ÷ weight in kg = mcg/kg/min
Rate from an orderOrdered dose per hour ÷ concentration = mL/h

Low-dose epinephrine infusion for refractory anaphylaxis (Resuscitation Council UK 2021): start it when breathing or circulation has not improved after two appropriate IM doses. Mix 1 mg of 1 mg/mL epinephrine in 100 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride, which is 10 mcg/mL, and run it on a pump through a dedicated line, not piggybacked onto another line unless an anti-reflux valve is used, and not in the arm with the BP cuff. Start at 0.5 mL/kg/h for moderate reactions (about 0.1 mcg/kg/min) or 1 mL/kg/h if hypotensive or hypoxic, and titrate to the lowest effective rate; steady state takes 5–10 min after each change. RCUK reserves IV epinephrine for experienced specialists in an appropriate setting; transport programs set their own scope for these infusions.

Worked example: a 60 kg patient at 0.5 mL/kg/h runs at 30 mL/h. At 10 mcg/mL that is 300 mcg/h, or 5 mcg/min, which is 0.083 mcg/kg/min. Keep monitoring with ECG, SpO₂ and a BP at least every 5 min; tachycardia, tremor or pallor with a normal or raised BP suggests too much epinephrine, so lower the rate. Until the infusion runs, IM epinephrine is repeated every 5 min.

Safety Common errors: leaving the answer in mcg/h, reading a 1 mg in 100 mL bag as 100 mcg/mL instead of 10, and reporting the per-kilogram dose when the order asks for the total.
